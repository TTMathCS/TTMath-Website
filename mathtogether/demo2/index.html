What this is

A club that makes math feel alive.

Founded by math enthusiasts, Math Together Canada makes math fun and accessible for everyone. We help students explore contest strategies, school support, livestream lessons, charity contests, and peer mentorship in a community that is serious about learning and generous with help.

proof sketch
  1. Find students who love hard problems.
  2. Build events where nobody has to solve alone.
  3. Turn curiosity into confidence.

Challenge arena

Livestreams that feel like a team room.

Math Together Canada invites students to live problem-solving sessions with contest breakdowns, strategy discussion, and Q&A on the YouTube channel.

  • Problem-solving. Tackle AMC, AIME, Waterloo, and olympiad-style ideas.
  • Interactive energy. Think in public, compare methods, and learn from peers.
  • Multiple levels. Build foundations or chase advanced contest insight.

FAQ

Math queries unleashed

What is Math Together Canada?

A student-led community dedicated to making math fun, accessible, and collaborative.

How can I join?

Use the join form and tell us whether you want tutoring, volunteering, events, or community updates.

What events do you offer?

Tutoring, contest prep, livestreams, charity contests, summer camps, and speaker sessions.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. Students can help teach, organize, design, write problems, run events, and support outreach.
