The main tent

Math Together is a student carnival for serious curiosity.

Founded by math enthusiasts, Math Together Canada makes math fun and accessible through peer tutoring, contest preparation, livestreams, summer camps, charity contests, and student leadership.

Students learn faster when hard problems become shared experiences. We build rooms where asking a question, testing a method, and changing your mind all feel natural.
