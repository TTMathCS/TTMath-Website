Problem 2(a)

Splitting the modulus · last two digits of a big power

49¹⁹ has 33 digits — no calculator will show you its last two. Try to guess them… then watch us pin them down in three lines.
Find the last two digits of 49¹⁹.
Try it yourself first — show the full solutionHide the solution Tool: "last two digits" = mod 100; split 100 = 4 × 25; use −1

The last two digits of a number are its value mod 100. We want 49¹⁹ mod 100. Since gcd(4, 25) = 1, split into mod 4 and mod 25.

Mod 4

49 ≡ 1 (mod 4)   →   49¹⁹ ≡ 1¹⁹ = 1 (mod 4)

Mod 25 — the −1 shortcut

49 = 50 − 1 ≡ −1 (mod 25)   →   49¹⁹ ≡ (−1)¹⁹ = −1 ≡ 24 (mod 25)

Glue with CRT

Find x < 100 with x ≡ 1 (mod 4) and x ≡ 24 (mod 25). From x ≡ 24 (mod 25), x ∈ {24, 49, 74, 99}. Which is ≡ 1 (mod 4)?

mod 4: 1²⁰ = 1. mod 25: (−1)²⁰ = 1. CRT → ≡ 1 (mod 100), so the last two digits are 01. Fun twist: 49¹⁹ ends in 49, but 49²⁰ ends in 01.
