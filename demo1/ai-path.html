Responsible AI Rules

School-managed tools

Younger students use approved classroom tools and capped school keys instead of personal AI accounts.

No private data in prompts

Students do not enter names, grades, faces, parent emails, or private school data into external AI tools.

Explain what AI helped with

AI-assisted work is accepted only when students can explain, test, and improve the result.
